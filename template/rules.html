Library Rules & Regulations

Online Library

General Rules

  • 1.Users must log in with a valid account to access the Online Library.
  • 2.Maintain proper online etiquette while using the platform.
  • 3.Sharing account credentials with others is strictly prohibited.
  • 4.Respect the copyright and intellectual property of all digital materials.

Borrowing Rules

  • 1.Students may borrow up to 3 books for 14 days.
  • 2.Faculty may borrow up to 10 books for one semester.
  • 3.Reference books, journals & theses cannot be borrowed.
  • 4.Late returns → Fine Tk. 5/day per book.

Use of Resources

  • 1.Computers are for academic purposes only.
  • 2.Unauthorized downloads/installs are prohibited.
  • 3.Digital resources must not be shared outside UAP.

Disciplinary Actions

  • 1.Any damage/loss of materials must be compensated.
  • 2.Misbehavior may lead to suspension of privileges.
Note: Rules are subject to change as per UAP administration policy.
