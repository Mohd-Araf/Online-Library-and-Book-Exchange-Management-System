Book Purchase & Exchange Rules

Online Library Platform

Book Purchase Rules

  • 1. Only registered users can purchase books from the Online Library.
  • 2. All transactions must be completed using the integrated secure payment system.
  • 3. Purchased books are non-refundable once downloaded or received.
  • 4. Physical book delivery (if applicable) depends on your location and courier service.

Book Exchange Rules

  • 1. Users can exchange books only with verified UAP Library members.
  • 2. Both parties must mutually agree before confirming an exchange.
  • 3. Books must be in good condition — torn or damaged books are not allowed.
  • 4. Once the exchange is confirmed, neither user can cancel it without admin approval.
  • 5. Repeated misuse of exchange requests may lead to account suspension.

Payment & Security

  • 1. All payments are handled through secure gateways.
  • 2. Never share your payment information or credentials with anyone.
  • 3. The system automatically logs every transaction for transparency.

Disciplinary Actions

  • 1. Fraudulent buying, selling, or exchange activity will result in account suspension.
  • 2. Repeated violations may lead to permanent ban from the Online Library.
  • 3. The admin reserves the right to remove any suspicious listing or user.
Note: All rules are subject to update as per the Online Library Administration.
